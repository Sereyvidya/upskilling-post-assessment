A NOTE ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

Built around a promise.

One exclusive offer at a time. The salon’s normal offer window is 15 minutes. The clearly marked 20-second demo makes the sequence easier to see.

Clients are fictional and every text is simulated. Accepting holds an opening; staff still need to update Square and then mark it confirmed here, including moving any existing later appointment.

Questions do not extend a deadline. Failed delivery pauses the search for a person to retry, skip or stop. Phone bookings cancel outstanding offers. Late replies cannot take a slot.

Search state and deadlines are held by a Temporal workflow, so reloading this page doesn’t restart an offer. This is a local prototype, with no public deployment or real customer data.
