BUSINESS RULES
1. Filter by requested service fitting the opening's duration, stylist
   preference, and availability; offer to earliest eligible waitlist entry.
2. One exclusive 15-minute offer at a time. Advance automatically on decline
   or no response. Decline/no response does not remove future eligibility.
3. Explicit acceptance only. Reject expired or competing acceptances even if
   no other client has accepted yet. Repeated replies must not create bookings.
4. Questions or ambiguous replies need staff attention but do not reserve the
   slot or extend the original deadline.
5. Closing the page does not reset a delivered offer's deadline. Show the
   current state when staff return.
6. Delivery failures pause the search for Lena/Carla to retry, skip, or stop;
   do not silently move on. Do not claim an unconfirmed offer is confirmed.
7. STOP removes the client from future offers.
8. Both staff members can stop an offer/search if the opening disappears,
   the original client changes their mind, or the stylist is unavailable.
9. Staff can record a phone booking, canceling the outstanding offer so it
   cannot subsequently claim the slot.
10. Acceptance holds the opening for that client; notify the client and front
    desk with service, stylist, date, and time. Display awaiting staff
    confirmation until staff have updated Square. Staff also handle moving
    any existing later appointment in Square.
11. Exhausted candidates -> unfilled, with staff notified. Appointment start
    -> stop. No separate travel cutoff was agreed; staff can stop earlier.
